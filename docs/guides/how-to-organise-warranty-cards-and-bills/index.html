How to Organise Appliance Warranty Cards and Cash Memos

The ultimate guide to digitising paper receipts before thermal ink fades, keeping serial numbers handy, and winning warranty claims.

Every Indian household spends significant money on home appliances, consumer electronics, and kitchen gadgets. Yet when an air conditioner leaks, a laptop display flickers, or a water purifier stops dispensing, an astonishing percentage of families end up paying out of pocket because they cannot find the original invoice.

1. The Fading Thermal Ink Problem

Nearly all major retail stores, supermarkets, and electronics showrooms print receipts on thermal roll paper. Thermal printing does not use ink; instead, a heated print head activates a heat-sensitive chemical coating on the paper. Because thermal coatings react to heat and light, receipts stored in standard drawers or folders typically fade into complete blank sheets within 6 to 9 months. By the time your television or washing machine experiences an issue in month 11, the store receipt is unreadable, and service centers will flatly reject free warranty repairs.

2. What Service Centers Actually Require

To honour a warranty claim under India's Consumer Protection Act, authorized brand service centers require: • The original tax invoice displaying the seller's registered GSTIN. • Clear mention of the product model name and purchase date. • The product serial number or IMEI number matching the device chassis. • Proof of an extended warranty or AMC certificate (if claiming beyond year one). If you have a clear digital scan of the tax invoice showing these items, service centers are legally required to honour the manufacturer warranty.

3. The 30-Second Unboxing Routine

The simplest way to eliminate lost warranty headaches is to build a 30-second habit whenever you unbox a new product: 1. Place the invoice and warranty booklet flat on a table under decent light. 2. Open Telegram and snap a photo directly inside @squaresvaultbot. 3. Gemini AI parses the store name, item name, purchase date, and amount. 4. Confirm the draft in one tap. The high-resolution photo uploads directly to your personal Google Drive.

4. Reclaiming Peace of Mind

When an appliance breaks two years later, you don't need to rummage through cardboard boxes. Simply open Telegram and ask: 'Show me the invoice for my LG washing machine'. The bot presents the original image, purchase date, and warranty terms immediately.
