What to Do Before Your Car Insurance Policy Expires

Step-by-step checklist to renew car insurance, safeguard your 50% No Claim Bonus, evaluate IDV, and avoid break-in inspection fees.

Allowing your car insurance to lapse by even a single day creates serious legal and financial liabilities. Beyond the immediate risk of police fines for driving an uninsured vehicle, a lapse can cost thousands of rupees in lost No Claim Bonus discounts and mandatory break-in vehicle inspection charges.

1. The 10-Day Pre-Renewal Window

Never wait until the final 24 hours of your policy to initiate renewal. Starting the process 10 days in advance provides several concrete advantages: • You have time to evaluate your vehicle's Insured Declared Value (IDV). Insurers often artificially depress IDV to show a lower premium, leaving you under-insured in the event of total loss or theft. • You can review whether essential add-ons (such as Zero Depreciation, Engine Protect, Consumables Cover, and Return to Invoice) are still cost-effective given your vehicle's age. • If your current insurer offers an inflated renewal quote, 10 days gives you ample room to port to an alternative provider without stress.

2. Protecting Your No Claim Bonus (NCB)

The No Claim Bonus is a reward discount offered to policyholders who made zero claims during the previous policy year. It scales progressively from 20% in Year 1 up to 50% in Year 5. Critical Rule: Under Indian insurance regulations, your accumulated NCB is valid for up to 90 days after your policy expires. If you fail to renew within 90 days of the expiration date, your entire accumulated bonus resets to zero. A driver who spent 5 claim-free years reaching a 50% discount loses thousands of rupees on future premiums with a single forgotten deadline.

3. Avoiding Break-In Vehicle Inspections

If you renew your insurance policy after midnight of the expiry date, it is classified as a 'break-in' renewal. Most insurers will not issue an active policy until a certified surveyor conducts a physical or self-inspection (video walkaround) of your vehicle to ensure no pre-existing dent or damage exists. This inspection process can take 24 to 48 hours to approve, during which your car must remain parked. If an accident occurs during this uninsured gap, you bear 100% of the liability.
