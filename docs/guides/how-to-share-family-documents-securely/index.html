How to Share Vital Family Documents Securely Without WhatsApp

Why emailing or WhatsApping sensitive identity documents poses serious risks, and how granular category access keeps your family protected.

Sharing personal identity documents, health insurance cards, and vehicle registrations among family members is a daily necessity. However, forwarding sensitive PDFs across WhatsApp groups or unencrypted email chains creates major privacy vulnerabilities and digital clutter.

1. The Risks of WhatsApp Document Sharing

When family members forward PAN cards, Aadhaar snapshots, passports, and insurance policies over messaging apps, several problems emerge: • Loss of Access Control: Once a file is sent to a group or chat, it is saved to everyone's local phone storage and cloud backups (Google Drive or iCloud backups), often in unencrypted format. • Version Confusion: When an insurance policy is renewed, older expired copies continue floating in chat history, leading to confusion during medical emergencies. • Phone Loss Vulnerability: If a family member's phone is lost or stolen, unencrypted document images stored in their photo gallery become vulnerable to identity theft.

2. Granular Category-Level Permissions

Not every household member needs access to every financial document. A spouse may need access to the family car records and health insurance, while teenage children only need vehicle papers when driving. In SQUARES Vault, we built Family Access Grants with category-specific boundaries. You can grant access to the 'vehicle' or 'insurance' category without exposing banking, tax, or investment records.

3. Zero File Duplication

Instead of duplicating heavy files across multiple cloud accounts, SQUARES Vault allows authorized family members to query and view documents directly through their own Telegram interface. The master document remains securely stored in the primary owner's Google Drive, ensuring there is only one authoritative, up-to-date copy.

4. Instant Revocation

If circumstances change, access can be revoked in a single tap from the Settings menu. The shared user immediately loses the ability to view or search those documents.
