For Busy Household Families

Stop digging through family WhatsApp groups for appliance bills, health policies, and warranties.

The Core Problem

Households buy dozens of electronic appliances every few years: laptops, TVs, refrigerators, air conditioners, water purifiers. Invoices are printed on thermal roll paper that fades to blank within months, while health insurance cards and utility bills get buried in disorganized WhatsApp family groups.

How SQUARES Vault Solves This

Family Access Sharing

Grant your spouse or adult children read-only access to specific categories (e.g. 'health' or 'warranty') so anyone can pull up policy details during clinic visits without multiple logins.

Permanent Proof-of-Purchase Storage

Snap thermal store receipts immediately. Photos transfer directly to your personal Google Drive so proof of purchase remains crisp for consumer court claims years later.

Annual Maintenance & Warranty Tracking

Know exactly when water purifier AMC filters are due or when extended warranty replacements expire before paying local repair shops.

"When our refrigerator compressor failed in month 22, my wife pulled up the warranty invoice from Telegram in 10 seconds. The service center replaced it free."
