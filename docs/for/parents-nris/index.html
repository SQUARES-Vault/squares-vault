For People Managing Parents' Documents & NRIs

Oversee elderly parents' health policies, utility connections, and property records from anywhere in the world.

The Core Problem

Children living in different cities or abroad (NRIs) frequently bear responsibility for their elderly parents' medical insurance renewals, pension life certificates, fixed deposit renewals, and utility dues. Coordinating document renewals over long-distance phone calls often leads to delayed payments and lapsed policies.

How SQUARES Vault Solves This

Zero Distance Vault Oversight

Upload parents' mediclaim cards, utility bills, and property papers into a unified vault. Receive proactive deadline alerts in your own Telegram regardless of time zone.

Continuous Health Insurance Protection

Senior citizens cannot afford a break in health insurance continuity. Advance 10-day reminders ensure premiums are settled before the 30-day grace period closes.

Simple Telegram Interface for Parents

Elderly parents don't need to learn a complex portal. They can forward a bill photo directly in Telegram; Gemini AI reads the due date automatically.

"Living in London, I was terrified of my parents in Delhi missing their health insurance renewal deadline. SQUARES Vault sends me alerts 10 days ahead so I can pay online."
