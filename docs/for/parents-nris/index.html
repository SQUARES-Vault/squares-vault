For Expats & Families Managing Documents Across Borders

Oversee elderly family members' health policies, utility connections, and property records from anywhere in the world.

The Core Problem

Individuals living in different cities or abroad frequently manage responsibility for their elderly parents' medical insurance renewals, utility dues, property papers, and identity documents. Coordinating renewals across time zones over long-distance calls often leads to delayed payments and lapsed coverage.

How SQUARES Vault Solves This

Global Vault Oversight

Upload parents' medical cards, utility bills, and property papers into a unified vault. Receive proactive deadline alerts in your own Telegram regardless of time zone.

Continuous Health Insurance Protection

Senior citizens cannot afford a break in medical insurance continuity. Advance 10-day reminders ensure premiums are settled before grace periods close.

Frictionless Telegram Interface

Elderly parents don't need to learn a complex portal. They can forward a bill photo directly in Telegram; Gemini AI reads the due date automatically.

"Living abroad, I was anxious about my parents back home missing an insurance renewal deadline. SQUARES Vault alerts me 10 days ahead so I can handle it online."
