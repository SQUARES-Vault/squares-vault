Frequently Asked Questions

Everything You Need To Know

Clear, honest answers about our security, privacy, and architecture.

🔒 Can anyone see my uploaded documents?

No. Uploaded files transfer straight into your personal Google Drive folder. They are processed in volatile memory for AI date extraction and immediately purged from the server. We never keep permanent copies of your files.

🤖 Are my documents used to train AI models?

No. Optical parsing and question answering run exclusively via Google's enterprise Gemini API. Under enterprise terms, your documents are processed strictly in transit and are never retained or used to train public AI models.

📁 What Google permissions do you require?

We only request drive.file (which only allows accessing files created by SQUARES Vault itself) and drive.appdata (for configuration sync). We cannot see, read, or modify any other files in your personal Google Drive.

🚗 Which document types can I store?

Everything you need for life-admin: Vehicle RCs, Pollution Certificates (PUC), Traffic Challans, Car/Health/Term Insurance, Electronic Appliance Warranties, Bills, Passports, and Home Contracts.

⚖️ Is SQUARES Vault legally compliant?

Yes. The service is built to comply from day one with India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act (DPDP Act 2023), the European Union's GDPR, and California's CCPA.

🗑️ What happens if I want to delete my data?

Simply type /deletemydata in Telegram. SQUARES Vault immediately initiates an irreversible 7-step cryptographic wipe: revoking OAuth tokens, purging all database rows, and clearing search indexes.
