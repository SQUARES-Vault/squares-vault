Envelope Encryption

OAuth tokens and sensitive data are encrypted with AES-256-GCM using per-user Data Encryption Keys. The master cryptographic key lives outside the database.

Full Data Sovereignty

A single /deletemydata command performs an immediate 7-step cryptographic wipe: revokes OAuth tokens, deletes database records, and purges search indexes.

Built for India & Worldwide: SQUARES Vault is designed from the ground up to comply with India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act (DPDP Act 2023), the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR), and the California Consumer Privacy Act (CCPA).
