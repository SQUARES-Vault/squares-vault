Honest & Plain Language

Privacy by Architecture, Not Slogans

A transparent breakdown of how your files, tokens, and data are treated.

1. Zero Server File Custody

Your original files are uploaded directly to your own Google Drive. We do not store permanent copies of your documents on our servers.

2. Narrow drive.file Scope

We only request access to files created by SQUARES Vault itself. We cannot view, open, or edit any pre-existing files in your Google Drive.

3. Google Gemini API Processing

Document OCR and date extraction are handled via Google's Gemini API in transit. For details on Google's API data practices, review Google's Gemini API terms.

4. Simple Deletion via /deletemydata

Typing /deletemydata permanently deletes all your metadata from our database, revokes OAuth access, and cancels active reminders. Your Drive files remain yours.

Common Inquiries

Frequently Asked Questions

Direct answers about permissions, Google Gemini API handling, and security.

How does SQUARES Vault access my Google Drive?

During setup, you authorize SQUARES Vault via Google’s official Device Flow. We request only two narrowly-scoped permissions: drive.file (which permits viewing and managing only the files created by SQUARES Vault) and drive.appdata (for configuration sync). We cannot view, open, or modify any other files or folders in your personal Google Drive.

How is my document data processed by AI?

Documents are processed using Google’s Gemini API for optical character recognition and key-date extraction. Processing takes place in transit/volatile memory. Please refer to Google’s Gemini API Terms of Service for details on Google’s API data handling terms.

Is SQUARES Vault free to use?

Free during early access. Core features (Limited AI document reading, vault, expiry alerts) will stay free. Optional paid plans may come later.

What happens if I decide to delete my data?

You can type /deletemydata directly in the Telegram bot at any time. This permanently deletes your user profile and indexed metadata from our databases, revokes the Google OAuth access token, and cancels all pending reminder schedules. Your original files stored inside your personal Google Drive remain unaffected and completely under your control.

Can other people see my documents?

No. Your documents are filed into your private Google Drive and associated with your unique Telegram user ID. Only you—and any family members you explicitly authorize through the Family Access Sharing settings—can search or retrieve your records.
