SQUARES Vault vs DigiLocker

DigiLocker and SQUARES Vault solve two completely different parts of your document life. They are complementary, not competitors.

The Fundamental Difference

DigiLocker is the Government of India's flagship digital credential platform. It connects directly with national and state databases (UIDAI, MoRTH, CBSE, state tax departments) to issue verified digital versions of government-issued documents like your Aadhaar card, Driver's License, and educational marksheets. Under Rule 9A of the Information Technology Rules, 2016, issued documents in DigiLocker are treated at par with physical originals by enforcement authorities.

SQUARES Vault is an intelligent life-admin assistant built inside Telegram. It processes all the non-government, commercial, and household documents that DigiLocker does not handle: appliance invoices, extended warranty slips, private health insurance policies, rental contracts, maintenance agreements, and thermal store receipts. It reads them with AI, extracts expiration dates, schedules multi-stage reminders, and organizes original files in your personal Google Drive.

How to Use Them Together for Complete Peace of Mind

The most organized households use both tools in tandem:

  1. Keep Government IDs in DigiLocker: Use DigiLocker on your phone when passing airport security or showing your Driving License at traffic checkpoints.
  2. Forward Every Commercial Paper to SQUARES Vault: Whenever you buy an air conditioner, renew your private car insurance, get a roadside PUC test slip, or sign an 11-month lease, forward the PDF or photo to @squaresvaultbot.
  3. Let Reminders Run Automatically: When your car insurance or PUC approaches expiry, SQUARES Vault notifies you before you incur a fine or lose your 50% No Claim Bonus.
