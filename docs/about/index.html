Creator & Purpose

Why SQUARES Vault Exists

A zero-custodian document assistant built on the conviction that your household records belong in your personal storage, not inside someone else's walled garden.

The Origin

Like millions of Indian families, my household managed documents across a disorganized web of physical plastic accordion folders, family WhatsApp groups, and unlabelled Google Drive folders.

The inevitable consequences followed:

  • Paying a ₹10,000 traffic fine because a roadside PUC certificate expired without anyone noticing.
  • Losing an accumulated 50% No Claim Bonus discount on car insurance because a renewal deadline slipped past 90 days.
  • Paying out of pocket for a refrigerator repair because the thermal paper warranty receipt had faded into complete illegibility.

The existing solutions had major flaws: generic cloud storage (Google Drive, Dropbox) is passive and requires tedious manual filing; specialized document apps lock your files into proprietary servers that you cannot easily export or control; and government apps like DigiLocker only store official state credentials, completely ignoring household bills, warranties, and service invoices.

I built SQUARES Vault to solve this problem once and for all: a bot in Telegram that reads any document with modern AI, extracts the dates that matter, schedules proactive reminders, and saves every original file directly into your personal Google Drive.

Core Architectural Principles

1. Zero-Custodian Storage

We never store your original files permanently on our application servers. When you send a document, it is uploaded directly to your own Google Drive using the tightly scoped drive.file permission. You retain 100% legal ownership and physical custody.

2. Transparent AI Processing

Documents are processed through Google's Gemini API for optical text extraction and structured parsing. Processing files exist only in volatile server memory during the analysis call and are purged immediately after ingestion.

3. Total Data Sovereignty

If you ever wish to leave, typing /deletemydata in Telegram halts all reminders, revokes your Google OAuth token, and permanently deletes all database rows and index records. Your files in Google Drive remain completely yours.
