Extended Warranties & Protection Plans

Exercise repair and replacement claims on electronics, appliances, and vehicles before coverage expires.

What It Is

Extended warranties, protection plans, and service contracts provide extended repair and replacement protection on consumer electronics, major home appliances, and motor vehicles beyond standard 1-year factory warranties.

Where people usually lose it:

Warranty agreements are often paper leaflets tucked into original boxes or third-party email receipts. When an appliance malfunctions 2 years after purchase, owners assume warranty has expired and pay out of pocket for repairs.

Critical Dates That Matter

Warranty Expiration Date

The exact date when manufacturer or third-party repair and replacement coverage terminates.

What SQUARES Vault Extracts

  • Product Brand, Model & Serial Number
  • Coverage Scope (Parts, Labor, Accidental Damage)
  • Warranty Policy / Contract Number
  • Commencement Date & Expiration Date

How to Store & Track This Document

Photograph the warranty certificate or protection plan email when purchasing the product. SQUARES Vault logs the 24, 36, or 60-month duration and notifies you well before coverage lapses.

Frequently Asked Questions

Why should I track warranties on electronics and home appliances?

Major repairs (such as refrigerator compressors, TV display panels, or laptop motherboards) are costly. Tracking coverage ensures you claim free manufacturer service before expiration.

How does Gemini AI identify warranty terms?

Gemini AI scans the document for model names, purchase dates, warranty duration terms (e.g., '3 Years Comprehensive'), and computes the exact end date.
