Extended Warranties & Annual Maintenance Contracts (AMC)

Exercise free replacement and repair claims before manufacturer warranty windows snap shut.

What It Is

An Extended Warranty or AMC (Annual Maintenance Contract) extends manufacturer repair coverage for consumer electronics, major kitchen appliances, or vehicles beyond the standard 1-year factory warranty. They protect against expensive repair charges for motherboards, compressor units, and motor assemblies.

Where people usually lose it:

Extended warranty certificates are often stapled to the original invoice booklet or emailed as an afterthought by third-party warranty companies. When an appliance breaks 2 years later, owners assume it is out of warranty and pay local technicians out of pocket.

Critical Dates That Matter

Warranty Expiration Date

The exact calendar date when free repair/replacement coverage terminates.

What SQUARES Vault Extracts

  • Product / Vehicle Brand & Model
  • Coverage Scope (Comprehensive vs Parts Only)
  • Warranty Certificate Number
  • Start Date & Final Expiry Date

How to Store & Track This Document

Take a photo of the warranty booklet or certificate when purchasing the product. SQUARES Vault keeps track of the duration (e.g. 24 or 36 months) and notifies you before the warranty expires.

Frequently Asked Questions

Can I claim repairs right before the warranty expires?

Yes. Having an alert 10 days before expiry allows you to schedule a preventive service check for noisy fans, failing displays, or battery degradation while replacement is 100% free under warranty terms.

Does SQUARES Vault link warranties to purchase invoices?

Yes. If the appliance name matches, SQUARES Vault's entity recognition links the warranty certificate to the original purchase invoice so you have both ready for service centers.
