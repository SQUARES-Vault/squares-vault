Electricity, Water & Piped Gas Bills

Keep proof of residence up to date and pay monthly utilities without late surcharges.

What It Is

Periodic utility bills (electricity distribution, piped natural gas, broadband, water supply) establish continuous proof of residence for banking, passport verification, and address change applications across India.

Where people usually lose it:

Digital utility bills are delivered via SMS links or WhatsApp bots. They get lost in chat histories, leaving residents scrambling to find an active electricity bill under 3 months old when visiting a bank or passport office.

Critical Dates That Matter

Bill Due Date

Deadline to pay without late fee surcharges or supply disconnection.

Address Proof Validity Window

Most Indian authorities (passport offices, banks, UIDAI) require utility bills to be less than 3 months old to qualify as valid proof of address.

What SQUARES Vault Extracts

  • Utility Provider (e.g. BSES, Tata Power, Adani Gas, MGL)
  • Consumer Account / CA Number
  • Bill Date & Exact Payment Due Date
  • Total Amount Due

How to Store & Track This Document

Forward the monthly bill PDF from WhatsApp or email to @squaresvaultbot. The bot registers the due date and saves the original PDF to your Drive so you always have recent address proof.

Frequently Asked Questions

Why do Indian authorities demand a bill less than 3 months old?

Proof of residence rules under KYC guidelines mandate recent documentation to confirm the applicant actively resides at the claimed address.

Does SQUARES Vault track historical utility spend?

Yes. You can ask 'How much did I spend on electricity in the last 6 months?' and the bot calculates the total from your indexed records.
